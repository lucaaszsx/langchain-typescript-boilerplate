# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is BetterUX Solutions?** BetterUX Solutions is a web solutions company founded in 2019, specializing in UI/UX design and front-end development for web applications.

**What types of clients do you serve?** We work with startups, small and medium-sized businesses, and enterprises looking to improve the visual experience and usability of their digital products.

**Do you offer back-end development?** No. Our focus is exclusively on UI/UX design and front-end development. We do not provide back-end, infrastructure, or database services.

## Projects

**How does a project start?** The process begins with a discovery meeting where we understand your goals, audience, and current challenges. From there, we propose a scope and timeline.

**How long does a typical project take?** It depends on complexity. A UI/UX design project can take 2 to 4 weeks, while a full front-end implementation may take 4 to 10 weeks.

**Can I request changes after the project is delivered?** Yes. Each plan includes a revision period after delivery. Additional changes outside that period can be requested as a separate service.
